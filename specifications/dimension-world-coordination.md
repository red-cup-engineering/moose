## Research interpretation

Obsmetrologia's distinctions must survive into executable relationships. A
positioned observation, its articulated claim, a predication, the retained
support/opposition, and a settlement are not interchangeable records. MOOSE must
consume the original Metrologic → SignCell → SignChain → SignGraph → SignCraft
→ CAIRN lineage instead of implementing another boolean knowledge reducer.
FOUR readings and categorical/sheaf descriptions are recovered projections under
their original assumptions, not replacement primitives from which to redescribe
the source theory.

The source order is Mark → Utterance → performed Articulating → Predicating →
Positing → Settling, with generated sites, closure and kinematics downstream.
The cited research gives a conditional Semioverse construction over generated
performed histories and evidence fibers: its reindexing must preserve the
original evidence semantics and satisfy its actual functorial/coherence laws.
The native generation of those fibers is expressly unfinished. This design does
not fill that gap by choosing arbitrary graph arrows or naming an interface
after a mathematical construction.

In the existing Thing implementation, a Dimension and a World are kinds of
Container. A World inhabits a Dimension by an evidenced relation; it is not a
subtype of that Dimension. An Actor's persistent protocol identity, a Thing's
content address, a human-readable handle and an institutional grant are distinct.
Equal text or reachable coordinates establish none of those equivalences.

The existing World carrier retains source, causal parents and batch position in
its observations. Readings preserve contrary evidence. This supplies a concrete
route toward compatible independent observations and explicit disagreement;
it is not a proof that arbitrary distributed side effects converge or that
all coordination can be eliminated. Claims of savings or stronger autonomy
require a measured original workload and independently received outcomes.

### Occurrences, horizons and modality premises

An original performed occurrence and its content address serve different jobs.
Two occurrences may contain equal bytes or settle to equal readings while
retaining different sources, parents, batch positions, observers or admission
witnesses. Replaying the same admitted occurrence must not produce a new act;
content deduplication must not erase a distinct witnessed occurrence. The
original producer, rather than a second MOOSE identity table, owns that decision.

A reading is relative to an explicit evidence horizon: the original producer's
selected source/evidence, currentness witness, interpretation and admitted
observer scope. That horizon need not be a global clock or a latest timestamp.
Missing, inaccessible, opposing and unresolved evidence have different meanings.
An authorized projection retains the distinctions required for its use and
states its scope without exposing evidence outside the observer's authority.
A result valid at one horizon does not establish current authority at an effect
boundary; the existing receiver must revalidate it against its present state.

FOAM means **Field of Articulation Modalities**. A finite knowledge-join readout
is one projection within that field. The source's recovered modal constructions
have distinct premises: `Closure/Modal.lean` uses specified finite-fibration and
modal-frame data, `Language/Positing/Quartet.lean` provides a four-corner index,
and `Language/Positing/ModalKripkeFourValued.lean` distinguishes accessibility
hypotheses. A supplied modality must identify its witnessed original
articulation, applicable observer/accessibility or closure data, original
operation and laws, and what its readout forgets. A protocol tag or FOUR value
alone does not construct that modality. Any modal transport across an observer
projection must establish the applicable preservation condition before its
result is used as an original-source inference.

The research's ghost-composition example identifies two distinct intermediate
source positions in a public readout, allowing a composite visible path that has
no original source transition. Consequently, compatible views or matching names
must not establish an actual World crossing, composable operation, shared Thing
identity or transported grant. These require original witnesses and receiving
admission. The same distinction applies when a reduced readout glues but its
rich source evidence does not.

`calm_fiber_merge_order_independent` concerns one `ModalFrame`, one valuation and
two enumerations with the same membership as the chosen cell's fiber. It proves
equal knowledge-join results in that setting. The same Lean file explicitly
describes `calm_convergence_facts` as a conjunction of facts holding in its
lattice model; the general equivalence remains open. These are inspected
checked-in declarations, not a new kernel-build receipt. Using them for MOOSE
requires the actual source-to-program correspondence and those premises; they
do not prove that payments, allocations or other external effects can be
repeated safely. Effect admission and once-state retain their existing owners.

## Architecture decision: move existing coordination to MOOSE

The incumbent arrangement places both the reusable World gateway and deployment
hosting inside FeltOS. MOOSE exposes only social views while the reusable
coordination relationship sits in its downstream host. This impedes a second
consumer from selecting that relationship without importing the driver.

Alternatives considered:

1. Retain that arrangement and add another MOOSE gateway. This duplicates
   admission, timeout, recovery and authority behavior and is rejected.
2. Move all World/history/federation code immediately. This could improve the
   ownership structure, but current FeltOS and CAIRN resolve different SignCraft
   histories. Those modules contain private archive, occurrence and derivation
   bindings. A superficially compatible move can split the actual state owner.
3. Move the complete existing gateway unchanged, then continue the coupled
   World-history migration through a single upstream supplier. This changes
   responsibility without changing the gateway's wire or effect semantics and
   is the selected first step.

MOOSE does not acquire a second journal, scheduler, actor registry or authority
database in this move. It uses the original native request/reply port. Node-only
coordination remains an explicitly selected package entry; portable Fetch views
retain their current dependency boundary.

### State and effect semantics retained

| Observation | Result and permitted continuation |
| --- | --- |
| Input or admission refuses before transport admission | No submission. Correct the stated input/authority problem. |
| Wait ends before native admission | `not-submitted`; the stopped attempt cannot later retain or deliver. |
| Native retention may have begun but is not observed | `indeterminate`, preserving original request identity. Recover it; do not resend. |
| Retained submission has no matching terminal result | `pending`, preserving the original handle. Observe it. |
| Original started/executing result is retained | `indeterminate`; no second execution is inferred safe. |
| Matching addressed terminal result is retained | `terminal`, retaining the original native result and reply address. This is not automatically a successful business outcome. |
| Result exceeds its original representation bound after an effect | Explicit error with original handle. Resolve representation/custody; do not repeat the act. |

Current binding, operation catalogue, caller, recognized session bearer and
authority are checked at the existing effect boundary. The result must bind the
original request, recipient, subject, session and logical state. A later session
may observe only through the existing host identity reader and the same original
bearer. No receipt field creates that relationship by assertion.

The retained constants (8192 input bytes, 24576 result bytes, 8 in-flight acts,
60000 ms admission lifetime and at most 10000 ms per wait) are inherited source
contract values. This move introduces no measured throughput or latency claim.

## Protocol and modality composition

ActivityPub++ is the source-owned composition of protocols selected for each
relationship. ActivityPub supplies signed Actor delivery and ActivityStreams its
addressed activity data; native A2A supplies its own Messages/Tasks/Artifacts;
CloudEvents supplies event context; capability and payment protocols supply only their independently
admitted effects. The existing inline-SEN envelope is a named compatibility
profile, not a mandatory universal nesting rule.

ActivityStreams `context` can project an already-defined World/channel context;
JSON-LD `@context` defines vocabulary interpretation. Neither field creates a
World, grants membership or authenticates a sender. A transport queue's accepted
submission must not be presented as a peer's acceptance or settlement. The
existing provider's directed-exchange and delivery-receipt proposals require
their own source qualification and receiving before MOOSE can claim them.

Correlation identifies a conversation or request relationship. Causation retains
the actual predecessor evidence. Arrival time is neither a causal total order
nor the semantic identity of the same observation replayed later. Ledgers must
retain opposed testimony and the reason an effect was admitted/refused rather
than laundering a transport `Accept` into institutional or financial authority.

1. Bind the original source bytes, declared dependencies, public package exposure
   and licensing provenance before relocation. The original gateway is present
   unchanged in the anonymously retrievable FeltOS `0.21.9-dev.1014` archive
   (SHA-1 `ed60612a00b0ceb7dc44fe7f9c74ae418abd2ee1`).

   original semantic-ID and JSON providers. Independently derive focused cases
   for valid execution, a materially different valid World, refusal, authority
   changes, late retention, lost replies, bounded concurrency and recovery.
3. Preserve the original MOOSE Fetch and social tests and qualify the packed
   export. Isolated port doubles establish only gateway mechanism behavior.
4. Select the published MOOSE edition in FeltOS and replace the original gateway
   implementation with the pure forwarding entry. Preserve its public export.
5. Receive FeltOS's requester and the existing MetroPlat receiver with the real
   signed provider, custody, account/office recognition and independent accounts.
   Existing `test/coordination/world-gateway.*` suites remain at their original
   owners. A compact MOOSE unit suite cannot substitute for those tests.
6. Observe the existing deployed host's actual selected artifact and original
   user's same-World job. Package publication, host installation and independent
   runtime receiving remain separately evidenced stages.

The relocation is a source-preserving architectural normalization, not a claim of
newly fabricated mathematical semantics. Candidate source, package tests and a
deployment receipt each establish only their own stage.
